Datta Advocates Privacy Policy

SMS Section

At Datta Advocates, we respect your privacy. We will only use information you provide through text messaging to transmit your mobile messages and respond to your customer service inquiries.

No Mobile Information Sharing: We will not share, sell, rent, or lease your mobile phone number, text content, or any associated personal data with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

Data sharing may only occur with trusted service providers (such as Microsoft Teams/telecom carriers) strictly required to deliver the messaging service, or if required by law.
